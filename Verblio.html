Questions, answered

Is Verblio really free?

Yes. Lessons, levels, streaks and leagues are free. Plus adds unlimited hearts, offline lessons and extra practice tools.

Which level should I start at?

If you are not sure, take the placement quiz when you sign up. You can move up or down a level at any time.

How long is one lesson?

Most lessons take about five minutes, with five questions each.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

A wrong answer costs one heart. When all three are gone you can restart the lesson. Plus has unlimited hearts.
